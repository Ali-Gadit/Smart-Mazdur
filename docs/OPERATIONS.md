# Operations & Pilot Playbook

## 1. Pilot Scope
- **Location:** Initial single pilot zone in Karachi, Pakistan.
- **Initial Trades:** Plumbing, Electrical, AC Repair & Maintenance.
- **Pricing:** Transparent visit fees for diagnosis + itemized quotes before repair work starts. Fixed pricing only for well-scoped, standard tasks.
- **Settlement:** Cash payment directly between customer and technician, recorded transparently in the platform.

## 2. Onboarding & Verification Procedures
1. **Technician Application:**
   - Identity: Manual review of government identity details (CNIC) recorded by admin.
   - Skill Check: Independent practical assessment checklist conducted by an experienced senior technician or trade partner.
   - Distinct statuses: Identity review and skill assessment are evaluated and tracked independently.
2. **Catalog Administration:**
   - Categories and tasks managed via data, not code redeployments.
   - Scope inclusions and exclusions clearly itemized for each task.

## 3. Support & Incident Response
- Dedicated support hours during the pilot.
- All dispute requests logged in `disputes` table with timeline, evidence, and assigned admin.
- Platform provides mediation; does not make false claims of third-party insurance or emergency rescue services.
